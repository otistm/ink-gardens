# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- A soft opening: a short, calm evening with Nana before the week starts. She walks you through planting, watering, picking, selling, pulling a weed and a two-flower order, one tip at a time.
- Nothing can go wrong in it. Customers wait as long as you like, plants never dry out and blooms never fade.
- Each tip has a ring that fills while you read, then turns into an x to close it, like Ink Crossing. Whatever Nana is talking about gets a dashed ring around it.
- New players see Soft opening first on the title screen, with Skip to Monday underneath. Everyone else can replay it from Play the soft opening.
- Doing things out of order never leaves you stuck: Nana just moves on.

## 0.1.1
- The lettering now matches the other ink games: Fraunces for titles and numbers, and Figtree for everything else.

## 0.1.0
- First playable version. Nana left you her flower shop and the garden behind it, and Everbloom, a plastic flower superstore, just opened across the street. You have one week, Monday to Sunday, to win the street.
- The garden is a grid of beds. One tap does the obvious thing: plant the seed you picked in the tray, water a thirsty plant, pick a flower in bloom, pull a weed or clear a dead plant.
- Eight plants, each with its own pace and thirst: daisy, tulip, sunflower, fern, lavender, rose, cactus and orchid. A new one joins the seed tray each morning.
- Plants only grow with water, and die if they stay dry too long. Blooms fade if you leave them on the stem.
- Customers wait at the top with their order and a patience bar. Tap them when the bucket holds what they want. Quick service tips more; slow service sends them to Everbloom.
- Between days, the seed shop: dig more beds (up to 12) and buy a sprinkler, a cold frame, flower food or a striped awning.
- Each day gets busier. Saturday is market day and Sunday is the flower show.
- Monday shows a hint line until you've planted, watered, picked and sold once.
- The week is saved each morning, so "Carry on" picks up where you left off. Lose a day and you can try it again from that morning.
- Send feedback from the title, pause and end screens. Crashes are reported quietly.
- Front page with a Play button, dark mode, and it installs to the home screen like the other ink games.
